A café for slow, cosy evenings

IX Coffee started as one roaster in a garage and a stubborn love of autumn. We roast every Tuesday, bake every morning and light the candles at sunset.

From October 1st the whole menu turns orange, and on the 31st the staff come to work in costume. Yes, even the barista who says he hates dressing up.
